01 / the pipeline

What I own, end to end.

  1. ingest
  2. parse
  3. validate
  4. enrich
  5. decide

  1. stage 01 · ingest

    Raw court-record XML, from many vendors.

    Every vendor sends records in its own shape. That mess is the input to everything downstream.

  2. stage 02 · parse

    One Python/FastAPI service turns it into structure.

    I own the transformation service that converts heterogeneous vendor XML into the structured features the platform runs on.

  3. stage 03 · validate

    Schemas and tests keep bad data out.

    XSD schemas enforce consistency on incoming XML; pytest coverage guards parsing, enrichment, and decision logic.

  4. stage 04 · enrich

    Bronze, silver, gold, in BigQuery.

    When production breaks, I trace records through the medallion pipeline to the root cause: parser failures, ID-mapping bugs.

  5. stage 05 · decide

    Automated compliance decisions.

    Jurisdiction-specific logic (offense age, dispositions, court exclusions) expands the share of records adjudicated without manual review.
